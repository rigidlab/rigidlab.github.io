# Is there software that Claude cannot build?
I was still an AI skeptic back in 2025, as you can see from [my previous post](../llm-bug-fix/index.md) back in December. The pace of improvement has gotten exponentially better since then, at least from my experience and recollection. I expected LLMs to improve the state of the art of AI-assisted coding eventually, but not this quickly. I don't want to say LLMs made me eat my own words, but it seems like they did.

<!-- more -->

On the plus side, it has enabled me to build out quite a few of my pet projects on the weekend with a few prompts. See my [projects](../../../projects/index.md).

On the minus side, it raises real uncertainty about job prospects for the next generation. Those already established in tech may be safe, perhaps for a few more years.

If everything and anything can be built or solved by AI without supervision, then what is the meaning of intellectual pursuit for lifelong learning?
